  MONITOR: tail -f data/logs/oct6-refresh.log
  ABORT SIGNALS: a 429 storm, or repeated "SerpApi failed". Ctrl-C; the ledger
  only records COMPLETED searches, so the next run resumes exactly where this
  one stopped. Nothing is half-written.
